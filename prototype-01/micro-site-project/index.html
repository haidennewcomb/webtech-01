Character Overview

The Lord of Hongyuan Identity represents a version of Hong Lu shaped by extreme privilege, power, and emotional detachment. Unlike his more curious and gentle baseline personality, this incarnation exists as a ruler who has grown completely accustomed to control.

His calm demeanor is not kindness—it is distance. Violence does not disturb him, suffering does not provoke empathy, and authority comes as naturally as breathing. This identity embodies the idea that prolonged exposure to power erodes humanity.

The setting surrounding him reinforces this interpretation: ritualistic, ancient, and filled with symbolic imagery of decay and dominance.

Core Themes

  • Detached Nobility: Power without emotional burden
  • Decay of Aristocracy: Beauty masking corruption
  • Control vs Humanity: Authority replacing empathy
  • Observation over Action: Watching instead of intervening
  • Bad End Identity(ID):The Bad End Identities (IDs) alternate identities where sinners fully succumb to their trauma, flaws, or environmental pressures

Notable Actions

  • Maintains absolute composure in violent environments
  • Allows chaos to unfold without interference
  • Commands authority without raising his voice
  • Views others as pieces within a larger structure
  • Exerts psychological dominance rather than physical force
